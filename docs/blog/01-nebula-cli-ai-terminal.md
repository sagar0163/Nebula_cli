# Why We Built Another AI Terminal Tool

*By the Nebula-CLI Team*

The terminal is the heart of developer productivity. Yet, in the age of AI, most of us still leave the terminal to ask ChatGPT how to reverse a string in bash or why our Docker container is failing to start.

We built **Nebula-CLI** to bring the AI directly to where the problems happen.

## The Problem
Context switching is expensive. Copying and pasting errors into a browser tab loses the context of your environment, your OS, and your recent command history.

## The Solution
Nebula-CLI is an open-source tool that deeply integrates with your shell. It can:
- **Analyze errors** natively by reading stderr.
- **Generate complex commands** based on plain English queries.
- **Run community patterns** to automatically fix common environment setups.

## Community First
